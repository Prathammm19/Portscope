Network Reconnaissance Console

PORTSCOPE

TCP connect scan + service fingerprinting, run from your browser. Only scan hosts you own or are authorized to test.

Why the guardrails: this console caps scans to 500 ports per request and rate-limits requests to prevent misuse as an open scanning relay. For learning port-by-port mechanics, run port_scanner.py directly from the command line instead — it has no such caps for your own authorized targets.
